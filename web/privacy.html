Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. This policy explains how FCon collects and uses information when you use the audio stem separation service.

1. Information collected

We collect authentication data through Firebase Google sign-in (user ID, display name, email). We also receive uploaded audio files for processing.

2. How data is used

Authentication data is used to control access. Uploaded files are used solely to perform audio separation and deliver the output files.

3. File handling

Uploaded audio is stored temporarily for processing. The service does not use uploaded content for training or analytics. Converted files are deleted automatically 4 minutes after you download them, or after 10 minutes if they are never downloaded.

4. Third-party services

Firebase Authentication is used for sign-in. Your Google account data is handled by Firebase according to Google's privacy terms.

5. Cookies and tracking

This service does not intentionally use analytics cookies or tracking scripts beyond what Firebase authentication requires.

6. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact the site owner or administrator.
